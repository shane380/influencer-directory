# Paying creators — how to use the payments page

*For whoever processes creator payments (currently Cherry). The short version of
this guide is behind the ⓘ How to pay button on the payments page — keep the two
in sync when the process changes.*

The page: **Partnerships → Payments** (`/partnerships/payments-v2`). It opens on
the **Outstanding** tab — your work queue. One row per creator. The **Due now**
column is what needs paying in this run (months at or past their deadline); the
small "total owed" line under it includes months that exist on the books but
aren't due yet — like the current month's commission, which is still growing.
If someone is not listed, they are not owed anything.

## The payment run

1. **Work top to bottom.** Rows are sorted oldest debt first. A red **Overdue**
   chip needs action. A blue **Scheduled ⟨date⟩** chip means an invoice has been
   agreed — pay on that date, not before. Amber **Due soon** means the deadline
   is within a week.
2. **Check the payment method under the creator's name before sending
   anything.** If it says *No payment method* (shown in red), stop — use
   **Edit** to enter their details, or have them fill them in from their creator
   portal. Never guess where money goes.
3. **Send the Due now amount** in Mercury or PayPal — exactly what the Pay
   button shows. One transfer per creator per run; the app splits it across
   months automatically, oldest first. Months marked *not yet due* stay on the
   books for a later run — don't prepay them (the current month's commission is
   still accruing, so paying it early just leaves a fresh balance behind).
4. **Record it the same day, in the same sitting.** Click **Pay $X** on the row.
   The form is prefilled with the amount and today's date — add the method and
   paste the Mercury/PayPal transaction reference, then save. This one step
   records everything: deal installments are marked paid, commission balances
   are settled oldest-first, and the row (and any Scheduled chip) clears itself.

## The rules that keep the books honest

- **Record on the day the money actually leaves the bank.** The date on the
  record is what the bookkeeper reconciles against the bank statement. If the
  transfer is delayed, wait. Never batch up recordings for later.
- **The unrecorded gap is the only window where double-payment is possible.**
  Until a payment is recorded, the page still shows the creator as owed — so
  someone else (or you, tomorrow) could pay the same balance twice. Send, then
  record, before doing anything else.
- **Pay the full due-now amount, not a slice.** A partly-covered deal
  installment stays owed until it is fully paid — the modal warns you if the
  amount matches neither the due-now figure nor the full balance.
- **Don't pay off the books.** If a creator chases payment for content that
  isn't showing in their balance, it usually means their delivery hasn't been
  ticked on the deal. Delivery ticks ("Content delivered" on the Deal tab) are
  Daisy's records — ask her or Shane to tick it, and the amount appears in the
  balance on its own. You record payments; you never record deliveries.
- **Use Schedule… for invoices with a future pay date.** The row then shows a
  blue planned-payment chip instead of festering as red Overdue, and the chip
  clears itself when you record the payment.

## When payments are due

Everything earned from **August 2026 onward is due by the end of the following
calendar month** (Creator Terms v2) — August's earnings are due 30 September.
The practical rhythm is one payment run late each month, when rows flip to
"Due soon" (7 days before the deadline), plus invoice-driven payments on their
scheduled dates in between. Months before August 2026 were due on the 5th of
the following month, which is why old debts show as long overdue.

## What the Monthly tab is (and isn't)

The **Monthly** tab shows one month's earnings against payments, for month-end
checks against the accrual report. It is not part of the payment run — you
should never need it to pay anyone. The accrual workbook download for the
bookkeeper also lives there.

## If something goes wrong

- Recorded the wrong amount or date: open the creator's **History** drawer —
  pooled payments have **Edit** and **Remove** next to them. Deal-installment
  payments are corrected on the deal itself (ask Shane).
- Not sure whether a payment went through: check the bank first, then History.
  The bank is the source of truth; the app records what the bank did.
